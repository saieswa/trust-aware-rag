GrowEasy Refund Policy (Archived — Effective 2019)

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of purchase. Refund requests
must be emailed to support@groweasy.example with the order number attached.

Late deliveries do not qualify for shipping fee refunds under this policy.
Customers experiencing delays should contact the shipping carrier directly.

Digital subscriptions are non-refundable under this version of the policy.

Refunds, when approved, are processed within 10-14 business days.

Note: This document is retained for historical reference only and does not
reflect current company policy.
