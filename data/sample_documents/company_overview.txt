About GrowEasy

GrowEasy is a customer relationship management (CRM) platform built for
small and medium-sized businesses. The platform helps sales teams manage
leads, track deals through a pipeline, and automate follow-up communication.

Founded in 2021, GrowEasy serves over 4,000 business customers across
retail, professional services, and e-commerce industries. The platform
integrates with common tools including Gmail, Outlook, Slack, and
QuickBooks.

GrowEasy's core product tiers are: Starter (up to 3 users), Growth (up to 25
users), and Enterprise (unlimited users, dedicated account management).

Customer support is available Monday through Friday, 9am-6pm EST, via chat,
email, and phone for Enterprise-tier customers.
