GrowEasy Refund Policy (Effective January 2026)

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the
product is unused and in its original packaging. Refund requests must be
submitted through the account dashboard under "Order History."

For late deliveries caused by carrier delays, customers are entitled to a
partial refund of shipping fees if the delivery is more than 5 business days
late. This applies to all standard shipping tiers, including Express and
Standard Ground.

Digital subscription products (e.g. GrowEasy Pro, GrowEasy Analytics) follow
a separate 14-day refund window from the date of subscription activation.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval and are
returned to the original payment method. This policy supersedes all
previous refund policy versions.
