Clinical Guideline Summary: Drug X and Drug Y Interaction (2025 Revision)

Concurrent use of Drug X and Drug Y is not recommended. Clinical studies
have shown a significant increase in adverse cardiac events when the two
medications are administered together, particularly in patients over 65
years of age.

Patients currently prescribed Drug X who require treatment with Drug Y
should be transitioned to an alternative medication under physician
supervision. A minimum washout period of 5 days is recommended before
initiating Drug Y.

This guidance is based on peer-reviewed clinical trial data published in
2024 and reflects the current standard of care as of this 2025 revision.
