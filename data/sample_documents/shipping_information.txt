GrowEasy Shipping Information

We offer three shipping tiers: Standard Ground (5-7 business days), Express
(2-3 business days), and Overnight (1 business day, available in select
regions only).

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on package weight and
destination. Orders over $75 qualify for free Standard Ground shipping
within the continental United States.

International shipping is available to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the
European Union, with delivery times of 7-14 business days depending on
customs processing.

Tracking information is emailed to customers within 24 hours of order
dispatch. Customers can also view real-time tracking from the account
dashboard under "My Orders."
